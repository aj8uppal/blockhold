# Blockhold: after the Seraph

## Recommendation

Make **boss hunts and hero specializations** the next content release. Follow with tower mastery and a small tier-six roster. Add a hero, tower family or map when it introduces a new decision that the existing roster cannot express.

Level 50 can be the start of tier six, but filling levels 26–49 comes first. The current game caps accounts at 40 and stops granting roster unlocks at 25. Extending the same XP curve to 50 would add 26,800 XP after Seraph: roughly 56 repeat Normal clears of a 30-wave map, without first-clear or other bonuses. That is too much repetition to serve as the reward path on its own.

This is a proposed content direction. This release implements Seraph presentation/targeting, softer Dawnfall/Eclipse signatures and range/placement clarity; it does not add these future modes or raise the level cap.

## What already exists

- Seven tower families, three heroes, ten campaign maps, two branches per tower, tier-five signatures and shard-bought ascension perks.
- Heroes gain combat levels during a battle and can buy three signature ranks. Those ranks currently scale effect, reach and recharge; they do not create a new playstyle.
- Four main campaign boss identities. Two more—Ossuary and Veil Empress—appear only at post-clear waves +50 and +60. The Empress has a second, grounded phase.
- Daily challenges, trials, Veteran crowns, the Long Night and post-clear play already provide foundations for repeatable challenges. More entry points into these systems are cheaper and easier to balance than several disconnected new modes.

## First release: boss hunts + deeper heroes

**Boss hunts, available after level 25 or a campaign clear.** Target a 12–18 minute encounter with a preparation phase, an announced boss mechanic and a final test of the defense. Players choose Casual, Normal or Veteran. Keep hunts permanently replayable; a rotating featured hunt can add a bonus without making the roster depend on a calendar.

Start with the two underexposed bosses, then introduce two new ones:

| Encounter | Clear warning | Decision | What a mistake costs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ossuary | A visible resurrection radius and marked remains | Focus the boss or kill escorts outside its radius | Another bounded escort wave; no infinite rewarded resurrection loop |
| Veil Empress | Two phase bars; wings visibly fracture before landing | Balance air coverage with a reserve for the ground phase | A short surge of escorts during the transition |
| **Prism Warden — new proposal** | Three shield pylons light up, linked visibly to the boss | Split the defense to break pylons, then focus the exposed core | A delayed vulnerability window; no permanent damage-type immunity |
| **Ashen Conductor — new proposal** | A road segment is marked for a pulse several seconds ahead | Reposition the champion and soldiers; use the recovery window for burst | Temporary disruption with a clear end time; no tower deletion or indefinite silence |

Every boss needs one sentence of useful counterplay, consistent shape/pattern cues as well as color, and a health bar that shows its next phase. Normal hunts must be beatable without tier six. Veteran can demand precise preparation and an advanced roster; validate more than one successful strategy per encounter.

**Hero specializations.** Unlock choices through distinct hero challenges, with account milestones around 30/35/40 showing what is next. Let players change their equipped specialization freely between battles. Keep temporary battle leveling, shards and ranks as the in-run investment, rather than selling the same permanent stat bonus again.

| Hero | Path A | Path B | Evolved signature |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aldric | **Bulwark:** protect nearby soldiers and create safer rally positions | **Vanguard:** mark heavy enemies and open a brief burst window | A visible protective standard, or a directional armor-breaking slam |
| Liora | **Hawkeye:** mark a priority target and reward precision fire | **Gale Warden:** control an approach and help expose airborne threats | A focused piercing volley, or a persistent wind corridor |
| Zephyra | **Tempest:** wider coverage against dense groups | **Riftbinder:** reveal phased enemies and support focused damage | An expanding storm field, or a clearly bounded exposure zone |

Give each path a distinct effect silhouette and ability icon. Keep boss crowd-control recovery intact; a specialization must not create permanent freezes. Unlock progress should reward completing different challenges with the hero, with credit for assists and successful defense—not last-hit farming.

## Second release: mastery and selected tier-six transformations

Introduce one clear mastery track per tower family. Contracts ask players to demonstrate its role: cover air with arrows, exploit armor with mages, hold a firing zone with barracks, support several towers with Beacon, or defeat separated targets with Seraph. Show the next contract and its reward after every battle.

At level 50, plus a completed family mastery challenge, unlock a **Mythic** upgrade permanently. Purchase it with gold inside the run. Prototype an additional cost in the 15,000–25,000 range, then tune it against actual post-clear income and time-to-purchase. The price is a hypothesis, not a balanced value. Start with two or three transformations and one Mythic per defense, including the shared co-op board.

Examples:

- **Seraph / Helios Engine:** separate beams charge a clearly telegraphed solar strike. It retains the direct beam identity, with a major crown transformation.
- **Seraph / Event Horizon:** bounded rifts expose phased targets and create burst windows, with the Void visual language preserved.
- **Barracks / Last Legion:** an active standard that relocates an elite squad and creates a temporary defensive formation.

These should alter timing or positioning. Avoid adding six more levels of attack damage to every existing branch. Boss hunts should create a place to enjoy Mythics; the ordinary campaign should stay balanced around its existing five tiers.

## Third release: expand the roster selectively

- **A new tower:** prototype a support/control family such as a Tidecaller that changes movement through a selected road segment. Check that it has a useful role beside Mage slows and existing traps before committing to it.
- **A new hero:** add a builder or commander only after the existing three have meaningful specialization choices. Prefer a distinct action to another ranged damage hero.
- **New maps:** start with one compact boss arena built around the new encounter's counterplay. A new map should change coverage or timing, rather than add another entrance to an existing layout.

## How to know it is working

- Measure whether players start another distinct challenge after unlocking Seraph, not simply whether they spend longer grinding XP.
- Record first-attempt loss wave, time to first mastery reward, hero path selection and tower mix across successful hunts.
- Pair bot comparisons with human play: a greedy bot cannot establish whether a boss warning is understandable or a specialization feels rewarding.
- Test empty, sparse and crowded boards; 1× and fast speeds; both hero paths; both Seraph branches; touch screens; co-op; save/resume and replay compatibility.
- Preserve readable targeting and short effects under heavy fire. A stronger unlock should never make enemy silhouettes or attack boundaries harder to read.

Ninja Kiwi's [official update notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=960090&enddate=1738710130&feed=steam_community_announcements) describe boss additions in terms of new tower-selection and placement challenges. My design inference for Blockhold is to pair new powers with new tactical problems. The proposed milestones and prices above come from this game's existing progression, not copied balance numbers.
