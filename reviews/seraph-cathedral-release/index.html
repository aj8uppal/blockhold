Power and tradeoffs

1,400–1,600 true damage every 0.5 seconds, range 5.8. Nearby enemies take 40% splash damage within 1.5 tiles. Hits ground and air, bypassing armor and magic resistance.

The fusion replaces both Seraph powers and their separate coverage. It retains the survivor’s ascension, targeting and sell value. There is no map-wide limit; each additional fusion requires another opposite pair. A Sovereign cannot be fed again.
