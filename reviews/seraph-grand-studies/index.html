01 / THREE COMPLETE SUITES

A guardian. A sovereign. A sentinel.

OPTION 1

Crowned Guardians

The closest continuation of Stone Seraphs.

Broad, ceremonial wings and a clear crown. Tier five gains a lower wing pair; Mythic gains a third pair and an elevated sun. Void becomes a hooded colossus with a split eclipse crown.

Full suite & motion
OPTION 2

Thrones of Light

Grand through breadth, posture and stillness.

A seated deity with solid feet, a sculpted throne and broad rising wings. Solar feels serene and regal. Void is a dark judge. Its wings open gently as the held light fires.

Full suite & motion
OPTION 3

Cathedral Sentinels

A guardian framed by monumental architecture.

Tall stone wings form an open arch around the figure. Later tiers add outer buttresses and a higher crown. Solar reads as a sacred hall; Void as a threatening gate.

Full suite & motion

02 / SOLAR + VOID

The Crimson Sovereign

When a Helios Engine and an Event Horizon both stand at tier six, sacrifice either to awaken the other.

Two Mythics → one fusionNo extra goldNo map-wide cap
  • Choose which tower remains. Confirm the specific tower that disappears.
  • A crimson pulse deals 1,400–1,600 true damage every 0.5 seconds. Nearby enemies take 40% splash damage within 1.5 tiles.
  • Range 5.8. Hits air and ground. Pierces armor and magic resistance.
  • Replaces Solar Strike and Event Horizon. Gains focused boss power; gives up two separate firing positions and eight Solar beams.
  • The donor gives no refund. Its plot and terrain remain. The survivor keeps its ascension, targeting and sell value.
  • No repeat feeding: two normal tier-six Seraphs are required for each new fusion.

Balance intentA powerful reward with a tactical tradeoff

Both complete Mythics cost 66,500 battle gold before ascensions. The fusion averages 3,000 primary DPS and 1,200 splash DPS per nearby enemy before buffs. It concentrates the pair’s investment and frees a plot, while losing their independent coverage, Solar’s eight full-strength beams and the two old special powers.

Its splash is deliberately reduced and cannot hit the primary twice. There is no additional critical multiplier, uncapped feeding or second capstone running underneath. These are starting values; longer endless runs will still inform tuning.
