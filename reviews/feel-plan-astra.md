**Blockhold’s biggest feel problem is unreliable visual distinction.** Some existing effects are bypassed; others announce outcomes that never happen. Repairing those will matter more than adding particles.

I audited `src/` and ran isolated execution probes against its material and projectile code. Player-experience conclusions below are inferences, not a measured phone playtest.

The foundation is substantial: pooled emitters, weighted hitstop, shake, damage batching, disassembly, priority audio, adaptive music, hero portraits, boss cameras and ambient motion. Touch enlargement is indeed [1.28×](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/units.ts:155). One implementation correction: the builder makes **separate lit/glowing geometries per part**, potentially two draws, rather than invariably one. [builder.ts:89](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/voxel/builder.ts:89)

The five defects that matter most:

1. **Threat colour and tower glow are silently disabled.** Shared templates carry `userData.shared=true`; `buildModel()` clones that flag without clearing it. `Enemy.applyTint()` and `Tower.applyTierPresence()` consequently skip the clones. Debris fading also skips them. Probes confirmed both cloned flags remained true. Players lose elite distinctions and expensive-tower glow; debris disappears abruptly. The separately constructed capstone halo survives. [builder.ts:98](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/voxel/builder.ts:98), [units.ts:278](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/units.ts:278), [towers.ts:215](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/towers.ts:215), [debris.ts:182](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/debris.ts:182)

2. **Some attacks advertise nonexistent consequences.** `explode()` returns for submunitions **before** its damage loop: cluster explosions produce sound and particles but zero damage. Separately, an orphaned `BoltProjectile` continually targets one unit below its current position. The probe remained unfinished after ten simulated seconds, at Y=−74. Players hear secondary destruction without seeing enemies weaken, while some magic shots never finish. [projectiles.ts:230](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/projectiles.ts:230), [projectiles.ts:98](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/projectiles.ts:98)

3. **Dense combat lacks a reliable attention budget.** At 2×, a nominal 220ms spark lasts 110ms, but debris lasts 2.4–3.8 real seconds. Every ordinary death still adds shake despite light hitstop’s throttle. HUD pooling limits retained elements, not active allocations; audio “displacement” removes bookkeeping without stopping sounding nodes. The eye gets fleeting contact cues under persistent clutter and camera movement. [particles.ts:194](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/particles.ts:194), [game.ts:277](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/game.ts:277), [game.ts:2350](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/game.ts:2350), [hud.ts:1246](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/ui/hud.ts:1246), [audio.ts:115](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/core/audio.ts:115)

4. **Investment milestones share a small, generic celebration.** Upgrades immediately replace the model, replay the same dust and `upgrade` sound, then scale in over approximately 167ms at 2× without hitstop. Tier five adds a flash. Hero levels reuse healing sparkles and that same sound; the portrait’s level simply changes. Players can miss the transformation they just earned. [towers.ts:374](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/towers.ts:374), [towers.ts:397](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/towers.ts:397), [towers.ts:602](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/towers.ts:602), [hero.ts:86](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/game/hero.ts:86), [hud.ts:414](/Users/ajuppal/personal/blockhold/src/ui/hud.ts:414)

5. **Hits and deaths underexplain what worked.** Resistance changes damage, but ordinary contacts retain the same white flash. Death force varies, yet `Enemy.die()` passes only magic-versus-physical flavour and no direction; fire/poison’s true damage becomes physical-looking debris. Every enemy also gets the same grey puff and `die` sound. Successful counters and killing methods are difficult to distinguish. Build these ten changes in this order. Durations and caps below are starting values for phone tuning.

1. **Restore material ownership — S.** Clear `shared` on both material clones in `buildModel()`; preserve the flag on templates and health-bar materials. Existing affix colours, tier glow, phasing transparency and debris fades then function. BTD’s colour-coded bloon identities illustrate the value of persistent threat distinctions. **Phone:** no additional geometry; restores disposal of owned materials as well. In `Tower.update()/fire()` and `Soldier.throwAxe()`, prepare the throw during cooldown and release at the arm’s snap: currently animation and projectile start together. Two impressive things to leave out:

- **Per-voxel destruction with persistent rubble physics.** Authored-part disassembly already supplies the visual payoff. Thousands of independently simulated cubes would consume the phone budget and bury the road.
- **A full-screen spectacle stack:** bloom-heavy attacks, shockwave distortion, depth-of-field and repeated transformation zooms. Even BTD added effect scaling specifically to make crowded screens clearer; 100 towers are not proof that every attack remains readable.
