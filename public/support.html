Support

Found a bug, stuck on a battle, or have an idea? Get in touch.

Every report is read. To help track a problem down, say which device you play on and what happened just before it.

Report a problem or ask a question Reports are posted on GitHub, which needs a free account. Please do not include personal details in a report, because reports are public.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. In the iPhone and iPad app, progress stays in the app until you delete it. In the browser, it is kept in that browser's storage for blockhold.app. Clearing your site data removes it, so download a copy first: open Help & settings, then Account, then Backups & older saves.

Does Blockhold cost anything?

No. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does it work offline?

The app works entirely offline. The browser version works offline after your first visit.

What can I play it on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 15 or later, and any modern browser at blockhold.app, on a computer, tablet or phone.

How do I get better at a battle?

Open How to play from Help & settings on the main menu. When a new enemy appears, its card says which towers counter it, and the Field guide in Help & settings keeps every enemy you have met. If a map is too hard, try Casual difficulty, or come back after a few upgrades from the Royal Armory.

Privacy

The app collects no data. The privacy policy has the details, including the browser version's optional online features.
